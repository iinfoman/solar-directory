Overview

Where the directory stands right now.

Needs you

Listing requests

Businesses asking to be listed, or claiming a listing that is already here. Approving a new request creates the listing; approving a claim updates the existing one.

Installers

Every listing in the directory. A listing with no website, phone or email is hidden from the public site until one is added.

Featured placement

One installer can hold the national top spot. Any number can be pinned to the top of their own province. Everything else sorts alphabetically.

National top spot

Shown first everywhere

Adverts

Paid placements shown in a strip on the public homepage. Turn the whole strip off without deleting anything.

Advert strip

Announcements

A single line across the top of the public site. Useful for load-shedding notices, new province coverage, or a deadline. Only one shows at a time — the newest live one.

Reviews

Ratings left by visitors. Hiding one removes its comment from the listing and takes its score out of the average. Nothing is deleted unless you delete it.
